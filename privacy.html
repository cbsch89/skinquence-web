2. The short version

Ordo Cutis collects no personal data, transmits no data, and has no server.

The app does not request the Android internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET). It is technically incapable of sending anything. You can verify this yourself in the app information screen of your device.

3. What is stored on your device

You enter the following information yourself. It is stored exclusively on your device, in an app-owned database and an app-private directory that other apps cannot access.

Product name, brand, noteRecognising your own products

There is no sign-up, no account and no identifier tied to you or your device. No usage statistics are gathered, no crash reports are sent, and no advertising or analytics libraries are included.

4. Permissions

PermissionPurposeOptional?

5. Automatic operating system backup

Android backs many apps up to your Google account automatically. For Ordo Cutis this is explicitly disabled (allowBackup=false), so your skincare data and product photos do not end up in cloud storage.

6. Backup export

You can export a backup file from the settings. You start this yourself and choose the destination through the system dialog. Once the file is saved it is outside the app's control. If you place it in a cloud folder or send it to someone, that provider's terms apply.

7. Feedback by e-mail

The settings offer a way to send feedback. The app merely opens your e-mail program with prepared text. Nothing is sent automatically. The prepared text contains only the app version, Android version and device model. You can read and edit everything before sending. Sending the mail naturally reveals your own e-mail address.

8. Sharing with third parties

None takes place. There are no processors, no third-party services and no transfers to other countries, because no data leaves your device.

9. Distribution

During the test phase the app is distributed as a signed installation file to a small group of testers. A later release through Google Play is planned. Neither changes how data is handled. Once the app is available through Google Play, Google's own privacy terms additionally apply to the download itself.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Since no data reaches us, these rights have little to act upon in practice — there is simply nothing on our side to disclose. Concretely:

  • Access and portability: you can export all your data as a readable file at any time using the backup function.
  • Rectification: every entry can be edited in the app.
  • Erasure: entries can be deleted individually. Uninstalling the app removes all data and image files from the device.

11. Children

The app is intended for adults. It does not ask for an age, because it collects no data at all.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects how data is handled, this policy will be updated. The date at the top applies.

Not a substitute for medical advice. Ordo Cutis is a reminder tool, not a medical device. The information in the app does not replace medical or dermatological advice.
